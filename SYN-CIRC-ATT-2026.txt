Circular (synthetic) on minimum attendance
R1 Effective from 1 August 2026, the minimum attendance required to appear in the end-semester examination
is raised to 80 percent for all B.Tech students.
R2 This circular supersedes clause R8.1 of the Ordinances and Regulations for Undergraduate Programme 2023 to the extent of the attendance threshold.
R3 All other provisions of the Regulations remain in force.
