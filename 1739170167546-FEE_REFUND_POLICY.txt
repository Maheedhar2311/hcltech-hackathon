1. Refund Policy for centralized admission conducted by
JoSAA/CSAB/CCMT/CCMN:
2. (i) (ii) (iii) The refund policy for the candidates who withdraw their candidature during the
various Centralized Admission Round and spot rounds conducted by the
centralized body for admission i.e. through JoSAA/CSAB/CCMT/CCMN will be
decided by the corresponding admission body/authority conducting the
admission.
The candidates who accept the seat during the Centralized Counselling Rounds
but do not report to the Institute at the time of Physical Reporting at the Institute
will not be eligible for any fee refund by the Institute.
The candidates who accept their allotted seat through the Centralized
Counselling Rounds and pay the Partial Admission Fee and report and join the
Institute and thereafter withdraw their seat at the time of Physical
Reporting/after Physical Reporting will not be eligible for any fee refund by the
Institute.
Admissions conducted by the Institute at the Institute Level (B.Sc.-B.Ed.-4 year
ITEP Programme, M.Tech. (Self-Sponsored) Programme/MBA-Regular/Self-
Sponsored Programme/Ph.D. Programmes)
The candidates participating in various rounds of admission will be eligible for refund
of their fee on account of withdrawal of their admission as under: -
a) In case multiple rounds of admission are conducted by the Institute at its own level
for any programme, institute shall refund the entire fee after deduction of Rs. 5000/-
to those candidates who surrender/withdraw their admission two days prior to the
last round of admissions for their specific programme. However, only caution
money (if any) shall be refunded to candidates who surrender/withdraw their
admission after the abovementioned time period.
b) In case only single round of admission is conducted for a particular programme,
candidates who surrender/withdrawal their admissions after the single round of
admission only caution money (if any) shall be refunded to candidates.
