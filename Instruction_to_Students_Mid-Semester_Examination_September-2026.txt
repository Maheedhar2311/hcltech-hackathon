Mid-Semester Examination, September 2026
Instructions to Students
1. Students are strictly not allowed to bring their bags and other personal belongings including mobile phones
inside the examination center (Main entry of the building). Students are advised to keep their belongings
in their hostel rooms.
2. Carrying mobile phones, programmable calculators, blue-tooth devices, smart-watches and other electronic
gadgets in the examination hall is strictly prohibited. Students found with these devices in the examination
hall will be punished strictly as per Institute UMC rules.
3. Students are required to report at the examination center at least 20 minutes before the start of the
examination. All students will undergo frisking by the designated frisking team before entering the
examination hall.
4. It is duty/responsibility of the students to make themselves well aware of Institute UMC rules. Students are
required to adhere to them strictly. Institute UMC rules are available on the NITJ website.
5. Students will not be allowed to sit in the examination after 20 minutes of commencement of examination.
6. Students are required to carry a proof of identity with them.
7. All the students are required to sit in the examination hall strictly according to the seating plan. Seating
plan will be available on Institute website and also displayed outside the examination hall. Changing seat
in the examination hall is strictly not allowed.
8. Students are allowed to submit the answer sheet only after the elapse of half time.
9. Students are required to ensure before entering the examination hall that they have no material or paper
relevant to the examination in their possession or notes written on any part of the body/clothing. They will
also show the allowed instruments/data books/documents etc (if any) to the frisking team before entering
into the examination hall.
10. Making an appeal to the examiner in answer book, tearing out answer sheet pages or any other such activity
is strictly forbidden.
11. Talking to a person outside the examination hall while allowed to go to the urinal or for drinking water is
forbidden.
12. Read instructions written on the first page of the answer sheet carefully.
13. Students are required to fill all their particulars on first page of the answer sheet.
14. Students must write their roll no on their question papers on the earmarked place.
15. Writing solutions or doing rough work on questions papers is strictly not allowed.
16. Students found indulging in any activity as enlisted above will be dealt strictly as per Institute UMC rules.
17. Students must write question numbers in the left hand margin. Only blue or black or blue-black ink is
allowed to be used for writing on answer book.
18. The answer-sheet must be delivered to the invigilator even if no question has been attempted.
