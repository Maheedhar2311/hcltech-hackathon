Guidelines for UM (Unfair Means) Cases
These will be Unfair Means regulations of Dr. B R Ambedkar NIT Jalandhar, applicable to all the examinations
(Mid Semester, End Semester, Carry, Summer/Winter Contact Examination, Make-up Tests,
Supplementary Examinations etc.) conducted centrally or by individual faculty coordinators:
1. What constitutes an Unfair Means (UM)?
A student necessarily need not be involved in cheating to be viewed as a case of UM. Intentional act or even
unintentional mistake of non-compliance of instructions/guidelines for the examination will be considered as UM.
2. Actions by the Invigilator on detecting an UM case
On detecting an UM case, the invigilator will report the matter to the examination office through concerned
HOD immediately and will proceed as per guidelines given below:
(a) As soon as a student is suspected by the invigilator, a member of the flying squad, or any other
authorized person of resorting to unfair means, the invigilator shall immediately take possession of
the student's answer book and any relevant material found.
(b) All confiscated items, such as papers, notes, books, electronic devices, or other prohibited materials,
must be signed by both the student and the invigilator. These items should then be sealed and
attached to the seized answer book in the presence of the student.
(c) If the student is found to have written something on their body or elsewhere, a photo should be
taken using an available camera or mobile phone. If the student engages in unfair means other than
possessing unauthorized material, such as talking to another student, attempting to copy from
another student, allowing another student to copy, or discussing answers with anyone outside the
hall, the invigilator must duly record the nature of the offense.
(d) The invigilator will ensure that the student fills out and signs the prescribed form for unfair means,
adding their own comments in the designated section. If a member of the flying squad or any other
authorized person detects the UM case, the invigilator will also obtain the duly signed comments of
that official on the prescribed form.
(e) After completing all the above formalities, the confiscated answer book will be marked as ‘Answer Book-
I’ and a fresh answer-book shall be given to the student for completing the examination. The freshly issued
answer book will be marked as ‘Answer Book -II’
.
(f) If the student does not hand over the relevant material and/or refuses to fill up and sign the prescribed
form/documents, the same shall be recorded on the prescribed form by the invigilator. In this case, co-
invigilator may sign as a witness to the event. In case there is no co-invigilator, invigilator will
immediately call the allocated point of contact in the concerned department.
(g) No extra time will be given for completing the examination as a result of this procedure.
(h) After completion of the examination, the invigilator will ensure the delivery of the following items
to the Examination Section through concerned Head of department in a sealed envelope:
a. The prescribed UMC form duly filled and signed by the invigilator and witness (if any).
b. Answer books, duly marked as ‘Answer Book-I’ (confiscated copy) and ‘Answer Book-II’
(freshly issued copy).
c. The confiscated material (Mobile phone, smart watch, headphones, paper slips etc.) found in
possession of the student.

The UMC committee shall thoroughly examine the UMC cases on the basis of the material and documents placed
and give hearing to the concerned student, the concerned invigilator(s) and other official(s), if any. It shall submit
its recommendation after laying down clearly the nature of the offence to the Director for consideration and
approval. Necessary orders will be issued to the student by Examination Section as per the decision of the UMC
committee duly approved by the Director.
In case a student is not found guilty, his both answer sheets (marked I and II) will be released for evaluation and
result will be declared.
6. Important
(i) The candidate can appeal against the decision of UMC committee to the Director of the Institute within
15 days of the receipt of the copy of the decision.
(ii) The Director may appoint a UMC review committee and may send the case to it for review of the decisions
of the UMC committee, depending upon the merits of the case under extenuating circumstances.
(iii) The decision of the UMC review committee will be submitted to the director for consideration and
approval. Necessary orders will be issued to the student by AD (Examination)/PI-Exam as per the
decision of the UMC committee duly approved by the Director.
