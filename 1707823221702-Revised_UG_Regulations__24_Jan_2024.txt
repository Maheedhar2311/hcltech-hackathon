B.TECH. RULES AND REGULATIONS
ACADEMIC YEAR
R1.1 Academic Year of the Institute continues for a period, generally from July to June of the next year, as
defined in the Academic Calendar. Each Academic Year comprises two regular Semesters: Odd
semester (July to December) and Even semester (January to June). Each semester is of approximately
18 weeks duration (with at least 80 working days in a semester)
ACADEMIC CALENDAR
R2.1 The Academic Calendar of the institute defines the exact dates of all the important events, such as
orientation, registration dates, last date for applying for additional B.Tech. programmes,
commencement of classes, Mid-semester and End-semester examinations, vacation, etc., during the
Academic year and is approved by the Senate.
ADMISSION
R3.1 Admission to any undergraduate B.Tech. programme requires that the applicant be eligible, go
through the laid-down admission procedure, and pay the prescribed fees.
I. Admission through JoSAA
Admissions to the B.Tech. programmes of the Institute are carried out each year on the basis of
performance of the students in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE-Main) by common counseling
through Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). For admission to the B. Tech. programmes, the
Institute shall follow the reservation policy as notified by MoE, Govt. of India from time to time.
II. Admission under DASA Programme
Direct Admission Students who are foreign nationals either residing in India or abroad, or Indian
nationals residing abroad may be admitted to UG programme under the Direct Admission Students
Abroad (DASA) scheme according to the policy guidelines laid down by the MoE Govt. of India from
time to time. A few admissions are also given through the Ministry of External Affairs based on self-
financing scheme as decided by the MoE Govt.of India from time to time.
III. Admission under Cultural Exchange Programme
A few admissions are offered under the Cultural Exchange Fellowship Programme of the Government
of India, administered by the Indian Council of Cultural Relations (ICCR), New Delhi through the
Ministry of External Affairs as decided by the MoE, Govt. of India from time to time. For these
admissions, the candidates are required to apply through the Indian High Commission/Embassy in their
respective countries.

IV. Admission under Study in India Programme
A few admissions are offered under the Study in India Programme for the foreign students under
Government of India Scheme to Study in India.
R3.2 The number of seats in each branch of the undergraduate programme for which admission is to be made
in NIT Jalandhar will be decided by the Senate of NIT Jalandhar.
R3.3 If, at any time after admission, it is found that a candidate has not fulfilled all the requirements
stipulated in the offer of admission, the Dean Academic may revoke the admission of the candidate and
report the matter to the Senate.
R3.4 The Institute reserves the right to cancel the admission of any student and ask him/her to discontinue

on disciplinary grounds. The criteria for the former will be governed by these Rules and Regulations,

R3.5 The Institute will follow the fee refund policy as notified by the Government of India from time to time.

R5.1 A registered student can apply [Form UG-3] for withdrawal from the semester on medical grounds/
other special circumstances. Approval of Dean Academic is required for withdrawing from a semester.
In case of withdrawal from a semester, a ‘W’ grade shall be awarded in all courses of that semester.
Such applications must be made as early as possible and latest before the start of the End-semester
examinations. No application for semester withdrawal will be considered after the End-semester
examination have commenced. Each application for semester withdrawal will be examined by the Dean
Academic and depending on the merit of the case, an appropriate recommendation will be made.
However, fee paid for the semester will not be refunded. The fee shall be adjusted against the semester
registration fee when such student registers for the same semester again.
R5.2 The Dean academic can cancel the semester registration due to disciplinary or any such proceeding
against the student. The Senate approval will be required for cancellation of semester. An ‘X’ grade
shall be awarded in all courses of that semester. Fee paid for the semester will be forfeited.
COURSE(S) WITHDRAWAL/CANCELLATION
R6.1 A student can withdraw from one or more registered Additional courses in a semester before the last
date as announced in the academic calendar [Form UG-3]. (This date will be normally one week after
the Mid-semester examinations). The course(s) will be removed from the list of registered courses for
that student for that semester.
R6.2 The Dean academic can cancel the registration in one or more courses of a student due to disciplinary or
any such proceeding against him/her. An ‘X’ grade shall be awarded in those courses.
TERMINATION FROM PROGRAMME
R7.1 A student is required to leave the Institute on the following grounds:
I. If a student is absent for more than 6 (six) weeks in a semester without intimation, his/her name
will be struck off the rolls
II. A student may also be required to leave the Institute on disciplinary grounds.
III. On having been found to have produced false documents or having made false declaration at the
time of seeking admission
IV. On having been found to be pursuing regular studies (leading to degree or diploma) in any other
college, university or an educational institution without approval.
V. On having been found to be concurrently employed and performing duty or carrying out business
in contravention to academic schedules of the Institute without approval from the Institute

R13.6 The performance of a student will be evaluated in terms of two indices, viz, the Semester Grade Point

time.
The SGPA is calculated on the basis of grades obtained in all courses of one semester, registered for in
the semester.
SGPA = ∑(Course credit x Grade point)
∑Course credits
The CGPA is calculated on the basis of grades obtained in all completed semesters.
CGPA =∑(Course credit x Grade point)
∑ Course credits of all registered courses

However, grades awarded for Additional courses registered for earning additional credits outside
the prescribed curriculum shall be shown on the Detailed Marksheet.

CHANGE OF BRANCH
R15.1 A student admitted to a particular discipline of B. Tech. will normally continue in that discipline
till completion of the degree. However, there is a provision for change of discipline of study of a
student after the completion of the first two semesters (1st year) provided vacancies/vacancy
exist(s) in discipline(s). The filling of vacancies for change of discipline shall be done category
wise (General/OBC-NCL/Gen-EWS/SC/ST/PWD or any other category notified by the Ministry of
Education, Govt. of India, New Delhi).
R15.2 A student is eligible to apply for change of discipline at the end of the first year through institute
ERP portal (after 2nd semester) only, provided he/she satisfies the following criteria:
I. CGPA for General/OBC-NCL/Gen-EWS category students: greater than or equal to 7.50 in
the first attempt.
II. CGPA for SC/ST/PWD category students: greater than or equal to 6.50 in the first attempt.
III. Earned credits at the end of the 1st year must be equal to the credits prescribed in the scheme
of first and second semesters of their studies.
IV. Any credits earned through summer contact course, shall not be considered for discipline
change.
V. Change of discipline shall be made strictly on the basis of inter-se-merit of the applicants
based on CGPA at the end of the first year to the limitation that
a. The actual number of students in the 3rd semester in the branch to which transfer is to be
made, should not exceed the sanctioned strength, and
b. Maximum 10% registered students in 3rd semester of the discipline from which transfer is
sought, will be allowed the change of discipline.
c. Ties will be broken by JEE (Main) rank of applicants
R15.3 After the upgradation of the branch, if the academic requirement of a B. Tech. student is not
fulfilled as per the requirement for earning B. Tech. degree of the upgraded branch, the student is
required to attend and qualify the necessary course(s). These courses need to be offered as
a. Credit based crash courses with the start of the third semester (in case a course is
prerequisite of 3rd semester courses), or
b. during the winter vacation by the respective departments.
The final CGPA of the upgraded students will be calculated on the basis of their grades obtained in
the courses approved in teaching scheme of their upgraded discipline. Rules for summer contact

Appendix-D: Guidelines for Make-up Examination
All such students who will not be able to appear in their Mid-semester examination on grounds of medical emergency/or
any other special circumstances, can apply for the Make-up examination. The Make-up examination shall be held after
the End-semester examination and shall cover full syllabus of the course. The dates for Make-up examination (03
consecutive days) are to be notified in the Institute Academic Calendar. An additional fee as specified by the Institute
shall be charged per course. Following are the guidelines for Make-up examination:
1. A student can apply for Make-up examination only on grounds of medical emergency/or any other special
circumstances.
2. The student has to apply for Make-up exam to the HoD within 7 days after the end of Mid-semester examination. No
application shall be entertained afterwards in any case.
3. The students residing in the Hostels shall first report to the Institute Dispensary in case of any medical problem.
4. The Medical Officer of the Institute should clearly specify the rest period permitted.
5. Referral to other Hospitals shall be made by the Medical Officer of the Institute.
6. The medical certificate to be produced for Make-up application shall have to be issued either from the Institute
Dispensary or from a Government Hospital only, clearly mentioning the rest period.
7. In case of any Competitive Examination/Interview/participation in any event, the student has to obtain prior approval
before the commencement of the Mid-semester examination from the concerned HoD.
Dates for online registration for Make-up examination shall be notified in the Academic Calendar. The students
shall fill the courses details on the portal, after evaluation of cases, the HoDs will approve the registered
candidates online, and the students shall pay the fee of the approved courses through the portal. The academic
section shall circulate a list of all registered students for the conduct of Make-up exam.
In case(s) of any ambiguity other than the above-mentioned guidelines, the matter shall be referred to Director by Dean

A Summer Contact course is conducted during the summer vacation (as notified in the Academic calendar). A Student
can register for Summer Contact course if being offered by the respective department. The DUGCs of respective
departments shall display the list of Summer Contact courses to be offered in a summer break before the commencement
of the End-semester examination of the even semester.

course(s).
2. Registration for Summer Contact courses is to be done as per schedule notified in the Academic Calendar
through online institute ERP portal form.
3. A fee as specified by the Institute is to be submitted by the students for registering in each Summer Contact course.
4. The maximum number of courses that can be registered as Summer Contact Course in one summer break will be
limited to 02 courses. However, students of the graduating batch whose academic programme is expected to be
completed by the end of on-going semester (8th semester) may register for up to 03 courses.
5. The total number of contact hours in any Summer Contact course will be the same as in the regular semester course
(minimum 40). The assessment procedure for the Summer Contact course shall be same as that for a regular
semester course.

Appendix-F2: Guidelines for Supplementary Exam
1. A student obtaining 'U' grade in a theory course may register for a supplementary exam in that course. The
supplementary examination will be held within a duration starting after the ‘Display of grades’ after the
regular end semester exams up to one week after the commencement of classes in the next semester.
2. The maximum number of times a supplementary examination can be taken for each course is 02. If the student
fails to clear the course in 02 supplementary chances, he / she will have to repeat the course as additional
course with 75% attendance requirement.
3. The grade will be decided only on the basis of the performance in this examination. However, attendance grade
awarded in that course during the regular semester will be considered. The students opting for supplementary
exam shall be awarded one grade less than the actual grade obtained and the highest grade that a student can get
through this option is ‘D’. However, an ‘E’ grade shall remain as ‘E’.
4. Registration for supplementary exam is to be done as per schedule notified in the Academic Calendar
through institute ERP portal.
5. A fee as specified by the Institute is to be submitted by the students for registering in each supplementary
exam.
